Frequently Asked Questions

How does payment work? +

All payments are processed securely through Stripe. One-time packages are charged immediately. Monthly retainers are billed on a recurring basis through Stripe.

What is the turnaround time? +

Strategy sessions are scheduled within 7 days of payment. Brand Story Packages take 2-3 weeks. Monthly retainers begin within 5 business days. Launch campaigns are scoped based on your timeline.

Can I cancel my retainer? +

Yes. Monthly retainers can be canceled at any time with 30 days notice. No long-term contracts required.

Do you offer refunds? +

Strategy sessions are non-refundable once delivered. For packages, we offer a 50% refund if canceled before production begins. Monthly retainers are refundable within the first 7 days.

What industries do you serve? +

We specialize in serving entrepreneurs, coaches, course creators, and service-based businesses. If you sell expertise, we can help you package and communicate it.
